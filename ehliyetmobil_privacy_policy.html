EhliyetMobil Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to EhliyetMobil. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. This Privacy Policy explains how we process information in our driving test preparation app.

Information We Collect

Information We Collect Directly

Subscription ID: An anonymous user ID is generated via the RevenueCat service to manage your premium subscription status. This identifier does not directly include your name, email, or contact information; however, because it is a persistent identifier, it may be considered indirectly identifiable personal data under KVKK and GDPR. See the "Subscription Management (RevenueCat)" section below for details.

Local Study Data: Test scores and study progress are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

Information Processed via Advertising Service

EhliyetMobil uses only Google AdMob to support the free version. We have no integration with any other ad network.

Ads shown in our app (in rewarded ad format) are non-personalized / contextual ads. This means we do not show ads targeted based on your interests, behavior, or demographic profile. However, AdMob may process the following technical data to serve ads, perform basic measurement, and prevent fraud:

  • IP address
  • General device information (device model, operating system version)
  • Ad view/click events (without building a personal profile)

Subscription Management (RevenueCat)

We use the RevenueCat service to process and manage your premium subscription purchases. RevenueCat operates with an anonymous user ID — our app does not send your name, email, or any other personal identifier to RevenueCat. Data processed via RevenueCat includes:

  • Anonymous user ID (automatically generated by RevenueCat)
  • Purchase history (subscription type, start/end date, renewal status)
  • Active subscription entitlements (entitlement status)
  • Platform information (iOS/Android)
  • Device region/language for pricing purposes
  • App version

Analytics Data (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our app. This includes:

  • App usage statistics (features used, time spent, session frequency)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, screen size)
  • General location data (country, region — not precise location)
  • Crash reports and performance data

Important: We do NOT collect email addresses, phone numbers, names, or other directly identifiable personal information. No user account or login is required to use EhliyetMobil.

How We Use Information

Subscription Data (RevenueCat)

  • Processing and verifying premium subscription purchases
  • Restoring purchases across devices
  • Preventing unauthorized access to premium features
  • Showing correct regional pricing
  • Ensuring a seamless experience across app updates

Analytics Data

  • Understanding user behavior and improving app features
  • Detecting and fixing technical issues and crashes
  • Monitoring app performance and stability
  • Making data-driven decisions about feature development

Advertising Data

  • Showing non-personalized (contextual) ads
  • Measuring ad performance and effectiveness
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring ad quality
  • Supporting the free version of our app

Your study progress, test results, and app preferences are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

Data Storage and Security

All of your personal study data, including the following, is stored only on your device; we have no access to this information and it is not shared with third parties:

  • Test scores and history
  • Study progress and bookmarks
  • App settings and preferences

Third-Party Services

EhliyetMobil integrates the following third-party services:

Google AdMob — Used to display non-personalized ads. AdMob may process limited data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Ad Settings: https://adssettings.google.com/

RevenueCat — Used to manage and verify premium subscription purchases. Operates with an anonymous user ID.

  • Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Firebase Analytics — Used to collect usage statistics and improve our app.

  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Data Usage: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

User Communication

EhliyetMobil does not include:

  • User-to-user communication features
  • Social sharing capabilities
  • Chat or messaging functions
  • Community forums or discussion boards

The app is designed as a standalone study tool for individual use.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We share limited data with third-party service providers as follows:

Advertising Partner (Google AdMob): Receives the minimum technical data necessary to serve non-personalized ads (IP address, general device information).

Subscription Management Provider (RevenueCat): Receives anonymous user ID and purchase history data to verify transactions and manage subscription status.

Analytics Provider (Firebase Analytics): Receives anonymized usage statistics to help us improve the app. This data cannot be used to personally identify you.

WHAT WE DO NOT SHARE:

  • Your test scores or study progress
  • Any personally identifiable information (we do not collect this)
  • Your data with any other third parties not mentioned in this policy

Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government regulation.

Children's Privacy

EhliyetMobil is designed for users preparing for driving license exams. Because the app does not collect personal information and does not require an account, it carries a low data risk. Nevertheless, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; this is a general assurance that applies regardless of our app's target audience.

Your Rights and Choices

Access and Deletion

  • Access your locally stored data through the app interface
  • Delete your data by uninstalling the app
  • Request deletion of your device ID from our backend systems

Ad Settings

The ads shown in our app are already non-personalized. However, if you wish to reset your device-level advertising ID or fully restrict ad personalization:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off "Personalized Ads"
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → disable "Ad Personalization"

Premium Version

Upgrade to the premium version to remove all ads.

Analytics Choices

Firebase Analytics is required for app functionality and improvement. Collected data is anonymized and cannot be used to personally identify you.

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns: v.tastemir@protonmail.com

Data Retention

The device ID is retained only for as long as necessary to provide premium subscription services. When you uninstall the app, all local data is automatically removed from your device. You can contact us to request deletion of your device ID from our backend systems.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Our Compliance Approach

This policy has been prepared based on the core principles of KVKK (transparency, data minimization, purpose limitation). Our app is primarily aimed at users in Turkey. Because the data collected does not contain personal information and no user account is required, our data processing scope is limited. We apply the same data minimization principles for users from the European Union or other regions.

Reklam Hizmeti Aracılığıyla İşlenen Bilgiler

EhliyetMobil'de ücretsiz sürümü desteklemek amacıyla yalnızca Google AdMob kullanılmaktadır. Başka hiçbir reklam ağıyla entegrasyonumuz yoktur.

Uygulamamızda gösterilen reklamlar (ödüllü reklam formatında) kişiselleştirilmemiş (non-personalized / contextual) reklamlardır. Yani ilgi alanlarınıza, davranışlarınıza veya demografik profilinize göre hedeflenmiş reklam göstermiyoruz. Buna rağmen AdMob, reklamı sunmak, temel ölçümleme yapmak ve dolandırıcılığı önlemek amacıyla aşağıdaki teknik verileri işleyebilir:

  • IP adresi
  • Genel cihaz bilgileri (cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü)
  • Reklam görüntüleme/tıklama olayları (kişiye özel profil oluşturmadan)

Abonelik Yönetimi (RevenueCat)

Premium abonelik satın alımlarınızı işlemek ve yönetmek için RevenueCat hizmetini kullanıyoruz. RevenueCat anonim kullanıcı kimliği ile çalışır — uygulamamız RevenueCat'e adınızı, e-postanızı veya başka bir kişisel tanımlayıcı göndermez. RevenueCat aracılığıyla işlenen veriler:

  • Anonim kullanıcı kimliği (RevenueCat tarafından otomatik oluşturulur)
  • Satın alma geçmişi (abonelik türü, başlangıç/bitiş tarihi, yenileme durumu)
  • Aktif abonelik hakları (entitlement durumu)
  • Platform bilgisi (iOS/Android)
  • Fiyatlandırma amaçlı cihaz bölgesi/dili
  • Uygulama sürümü

Analitik Verileri (Firebase Analytics)

Kullanıcıların uygulamamızla nasıl etkileşim kurduğunu anlamak için Firebase Analytics kullanıyoruz. Bu şunları içerir:

  • Uygulama kullanım istatistikleri (kullanılan özellikler, harcanan süre, oturum sıklığı)
  • Cihaz bilgileri (cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü, ekran boyutu)
  • Genel konum verileri (ülke, bölge — kesin konum değil)
  • Kilitlenme raporları ve performans verileri

Önemli: E-posta adresi, telefon numarası, isim veya doğrudan tanımlanabilir diğer kişisel bilgiler TOPLAMIYORUZ. EhliyetMobil'i kullanmak için kullanıcı hesabı veya giriş yapma gerekmez.
